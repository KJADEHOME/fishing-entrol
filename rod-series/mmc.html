Purpose-built lure actions

Ten models span 1.6–3.0 m with lure ratings from 3 g to 50 g and line ratings 2–25 lb. Fast-taper tips give precise lure control and long, accurate casts for bass, trout, perch and shore lure work.

OEM note: private label + custom colour

Travel-friendly multi-piece builds
