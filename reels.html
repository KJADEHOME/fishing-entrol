Buyer questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you supply spinning and baitcasting reels alongside rods?

Yes. We source spinning and baitcasting reels in the classes most often paired with our rod programs, including low-profile baitcasting and large-size spinning reels for coastal and heavy-cover work. Tell us the rod model and target species and we will propose matching reel classes.

Can reels be ordered under my own brand?

Yes. Reels can be supplied with your branding subject to the model's minimum and packaging options. Send your artwork, target size and estimated quantity and we will confirm what is available.

What information should I send to get reel options?

Send the reel class or size, gear ratio preference, target species, estimated quantity and destination market. We reply within one business day with a product-specific quotation; WhatsApp is the fastest channel.

Can I mix reel sizes in one container?

Yes. Buyers often combine several reel sizes in one order. Minimums are confirmed per reel model; the exact mix and carton configuration are discussed against your selection.
