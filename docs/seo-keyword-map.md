## Editorial rules

- One primary search intent per page; avoid publishing a second page targeting the same query.
- Write for a buyer decision first. Keywords belong naturally in the title, first paragraph, headings and internal-link text.
- Every guide links to at least one relevant product page and one quotation path.
- Add only claims that can be supported by the current product specification, supplier evidence or clearly qualified general guidance.
- Review Search Console queries monthly before choosing the next article. Traffic, ranking and inquiries are outcomes to measure, not promises.
